What we do

Indian-inspired food for birthdays, weddings, office events and everything in between. Every menu is made to order in The Hague (Den Haag) and served across the Netherlands.

  • Stylish grazing tables

    Generous, styled spreads of savoury bites, sweets and flowers, set up on site for your guests to graze through.

  • High-end catering

    Full catering for private and corporate events, with menus built around your guests and your occasion.

  • Live chef experience

    A chef cooks at your event, so guests gather around the pan and eat it fresh.

  • Experiential dining

    Hosted dinners where the table, the courses and the stories behind each dish are part of the evening.

  • Custom party cakes

    Celebration cakes designed around your theme, plus Iyengar bakery classics.

  • Corporate bite boxes

    Individually packed boxes for meetings, launches and team celebrations, ready to hand out.
